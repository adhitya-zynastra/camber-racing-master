It's been a while since we've had normalcy in our lives. Well, unless you count this, our new normal. I've lost track, as many of you probably have, of the amount of time I've spent in this strange, yet familiar situation. Google tells me that it began on March 25th, but it feels shorter and longer than nearly 4 months. When we left the college, I remember our last discussion in person. The management batch gathered in the morning and discussed what could be achieved during this short vacation of what was likely to be 15 days. We were in panic at the thought. Two weeks was invaluable time and we had an event in 3 months.
